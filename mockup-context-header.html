Context header — 3 options

Each mockup is interactive. Use the toggle to simulate "scrolled" vs "at top" states.

A Collapsing sticky app bar

How it works: The app title bar and the context bar merge into one sticky strip. When "scrolled", the full inputs shrink away and two inline chips appear in their place. Clicking either chip scrolls you back to the top (and in production, restores focus to that input). Single-level — no expansion; the chips are the whole story.

B Sticky bar with chevron expand/collapse

How it works: A single sticky bar always shows the app name + two pill-shaped value badges. A chevron on the right expands the bar downward to reveal the full inputs in-place — no page scroll needed. Collapsing snaps it back to one line. Good for users who want to tweak values without losing their scroll position.

C Floating badge (no sticky bar)

How it works: The full inputs live at the top of the page (no sticky). When "scrolled" a small floating badge appears in the top-right corner showing both values. Clicking it scrolls to the top so the user can edit. The badge is unobtrusive and doesn't steal any vertical space from the charts. Good for desktop-first where screen real estate matters most.
